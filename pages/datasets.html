What the columns mean

  • Applicant: an invented identifier, not a real person.
  • Rent paid on time (of 12): the number of the last 12 monthly rent payments made on or before the due date.
  • Utilities paid on time (of 12): the same measure for utility bills.
  • Failed subscription payments: how many recurring payments failed in the same 12 months.

How the bands are used in this illustration

  • Higher: a score of 650 or more. In the illustration, a reviewer would still confirm the data.
  • Medium: 600 to 649. The illustration treats these as cases where a person looks at the full picture.
  • Lower: below 600. The illustration sends these to human review, and the score is not a decision to decline.

How these tables fit together

Dataset 1 holds the records, Dataset 2 holds the rule, and Dataset 3 shows the result of applying the rule to the records. Changing the rule or the records changes the scores, which is the point: the output depends on choices people made.
